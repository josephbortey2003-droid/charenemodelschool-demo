Questions parents ask

What's the youngest age you take?

We enrol children from three months old in our crèche.

Do you close for the holidays?

No. Charene is a non-vacation school, so care and learning continue through the school holidays.

Do you offer after-school care?

Yes. Children can stay after classes for supervised homework and play until pick-up.

Is there a weekend school?

Yes, we run a weekend school. Message us for days and times.

Where are you?

Madina New Estates, Accra. Call 055 901 6455 for directions.
